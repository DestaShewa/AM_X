Project Objectives

We divide objectives into business objectives and system objectives.

Business objectives
Validate demand for coordinated Arba Minch experiences.
Establish relationships with local tourism providers.
Create standardized tourism packages.
Generate revenue from successful bookings.
Build customer trust through verified providers and clear quotations.
Learn the real operational workflow before introducing automation.
System objectives
Allow visitors to submit trip inquiries.
Allow AMX administrators to manage inquiries.
Maintain provider information.
Manage tourism packages.
Create quotations.
Track booking status.
Record payments.
Manage follow-ups.
Record reviews and complaints.
Provide operational information needed to deliver trips.
