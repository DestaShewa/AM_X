1.4 Problem Statement

For the formal project documentation, we will write the problem statement clearly.

AMX Problem Statement

Visitors seeking experiences in and around Arba Minch may need to coordinate multiple tourism services independently, including transportation, guides, activities, accommodation, and local providers. This can create uncertainty regarding availability, pricing, coordination, and service reliability. Local tourism providers may also lack an efficient digital channel for coordinated customer acquisition and trip organization. AMX aims to address this coordination gap by providing a verified, human-supported service that organizes selected Arba Minch experiences through a single inquiry and quotation workflow.

1.5 Proposed Solution

AMX will provide:

A web-based tour-coordination service where visitors submit their travel requirements and AMX coordinates verified local providers to prepare a clear experience and quotation.

Core promise

Tell us your date, group size, budget, and interests. AMX will organize a verified Arba Minch experience with a clear price and local support.
