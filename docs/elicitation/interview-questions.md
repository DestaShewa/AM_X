# AMX Requirements Elicitation — Interview Questions

**Project:** AMX — Arba Minch Experiences
**Phase:** Phase 2 — Requirements Engineering
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. General Questions

1. What tourism services do you currently provide?
2. Who are your typical customers?
3. How do customers currently contact you?
4. What are the most common customer problems?
5. What information do you need before accepting a booking?
6. How do you determine your price?
7. How do you confirm availability?
8. What causes cancellations or problems?
9. How do you currently record bookings?
10. What would make coordination easier?

## 2. Provider Questions

1. What services do you provide?
2. What areas/destinations do you cover?
3. How far in advance do customers normally book?
4. How do you communicate availability?
5. What information do you need from AMX?
6. How often do prices change?
7. Do you offer group prices?
8. What cancellation conditions do you use?
9. What payment method do you prefer?
10. What commission/referral arrangement would be acceptable?
11. What licenses or certifications apply to your service?
12. What emergency procedures do you have?

## 3. Customer Questions

1. How do you normally plan a trip to Arba Minch?
2. What information is most important before booking?
3. What is difficult about arranging a trip?
4. How do you prefer to communicate?
5. Would you prefer one quotation for multiple services?
6. What information should a quotation contain?
7. How do you prefer to pay?
8. What cancellation conditions would you expect?
9. What would make you trust AMX?
10. What would make you recommend AMX?

## 4. Hotel Questions

1. How do guests currently arrange activities?
2. What tourism services do guests commonly request?
3. How can AMX coordinate with your hotel?
4. How is availability communicated?
5. What referral/commission model is acceptable?
6. What information should AMX provide before referring a guest?

## 5. Operational Questions

1. What happens from the first inquiry until trip completion?
2. Who makes each decision?
3. Where are prices recorded?
4. How is provider availability confirmed?
5. How are changes communicated?
6. How are cancellations recorded?
7. How are complaints handled?
8. How is revenue calculated?

## 6. Legal and Risk Questions

1. What licenses or permissions are required?
2. Who is responsible for each service?
3. What liability issues exist?
4. What customer information must be protected?
5. What payment/refund rules apply?

## 7. Closing Questions

1. What is the biggest problem in the current process?
2. What should AMX solve first?
3. What should AMX avoid?
4. What feature would provide the most value?
5. Is there anything important we have not discussed?
