# AMX User Requirements

## 1. Visitor Requirements

### UR-01

The visitor shall be able to view AMX services and available experiences.

### UR-02

The visitor shall be able to understand package inclusions, exclusions, duration, and basic conditions.

### UR-03

The visitor shall be able to submit a travel inquiry.

### UR-04

The visitor shall be able to provide travel date, group size, budget, interests, and service requirements.

### UR-05

The visitor shall be able to contact AMX through available communication channels.

### UR-06

The visitor shall receive a clear quotation before confirming a booking.

### UR-07

The visitor shall be able to confirm or decline a quotation.

### UR-08

The visitor shall receive relevant booking information.

### UR-09

The visitor shall be able to provide feedback after the experience.

## 2. AMX Admin Requirements

### UR-10

The administrator shall be able to view and manage inquiries.

### UR-11

The administrator shall be able to manage customer information.

### UR-12

The administrator shall be able to manage provider information.

### UR-13

The administrator shall be able to manage packages.

### UR-14

The administrator shall be able to create and manage quotations.

### UR-15

The administrator shall be able to manage booking status.

### UR-16

The administrator shall be able to record payments and financial information.

### UR-17

The administrator shall be able to manage follow-ups.

### UR-18

The administrator shall be able to record reviews and complaints.

## 3. Provider Requirements

Providers shall not require a self-service account in the MVP.

AMX shall communicate with providers manually using phone, WhatsApp, email, or other agreed methods.

## 4. Group Customer Requirements

Schools, universities, NGOs, and companies shall be able to request coordinated group experiences.

## 5. Future Requirements

Provider dashboards, online customer accounts, automated availability, and online payment integration are outside the initial MVP.
