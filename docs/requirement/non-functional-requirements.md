# AMX Non-Functional Requirements

## NFR-01 — Performance

Public pages and normal administrative operations should respond quickly under expected MVP traffic.

## NFR-02 — Security

The system shall protect administrative accounts and sensitive customer information through secure authentication, authorization, and secure data handling.

## NFR-03 — Availability

The deployed system should be available during normal operational periods with reasonable service reliability.

## NFR-04 — Usability

The public website shall be simple enough for a visitor to understand the service and submit an inquiry without technical knowledge.

## NFR-05 — Mobile Responsiveness

The public website shall work effectively on smartphones, tablets, and desktop devices.

## NFR-06 — Reliability

Important customer, quotation, booking, and payment records shall not be lost during normal operation.

## NFR-07 — Maintainability

The system shall use a modular structure that allows future features to be added without unnecessary architectural complexity.

## NFR-08 — Scalability

The architecture shall allow AMX to grow beyond the initial MVP without requiring an immediate complete rewrite.

## NFR-09 — Backup and Recovery

Important database information shall be backed up regularly, with a defined recovery process.

## NFR-10 — Data Privacy

The system shall collect only information necessary for AMX operations and restrict access to sensitive information.

## NFR-11 — Compatibility

The public website shall support modern major web browsers.

## NFR-12 — Accessibility

The website should follow practical accessibility principles including readable text, clear navigation, appropriate labels, and usable forms.

## NFR-13 — Observability

The deployed application shall provide appropriate logging and error monitoring for operational troubleshooting.

## NFR-14 — SEO

Public service and package pages should use appropriate metadata, semantic content, and search-engine-friendly URLs.

## NFR-15 — Localization

The system should support English initially and allow practical future support for Amharic.
