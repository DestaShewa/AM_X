# AMX Business Requirements

**Project:** AMX — Arba Minch Experiences
**Phase:** Phase 2 — Requirements Engineering
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft

## BR-01 — Provide Coordinated Tourism Services

AMX shall provide a coordinated tourism service that helps visitors organize experiences in and around Arba Minch.

## BR-02 — Support Complete Experiences

AMX shall coordinate relevant services such as transportation, guides, activities, accommodation, and other approved services where required.

## BR-03 — Provide Clear Quotations

AMX shall provide customers with a clear quotation before booking confirmation.

## BR-04 — Verify Providers

AMX shall verify providers according to defined verification criteria before representing them as verified providers.

## BR-05 — Manage Customer Inquiries

AMX shall capture and manage customer travel requests from initial inquiry through completion.

## BR-06 — Manage Bookings

AMX shall track bookings from confirmation through completion or cancellation.

## BR-07 — Track Financial Results

AMX shall record relevant prices, payments, costs, commissions, and revenue information.

## BR-08 — Provide Customer Support

AMX shall provide customers with a clear communication channel before and during coordinated experiences.

## BR-09 — Collect Customer Feedback

AMX shall collect and record customer reviews, complaints, and service feedback.

## BR-10 — Validate the Business Model

The MVP shall enable AMX to determine whether customers will pay for coordinated tourism experiences and whether the service can generate sustainable revenue.

## BR-11 — Start with Arba Minch

The MVP shall focus on Arba Minch and selected nearby destinations rather than attempting to operate as a nationwide tourism marketplace.

## BR-12 — Maintain Operational Simplicity

The MVP shall prioritize manual provider communication and operational reliability over complex automation.
