# AMX Stakeholder Analysis

**Project:** AMX — Arba Minch Experiences
**Phase:** Phase 2 — Requirements Engineering
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies AMX stakeholders, their interests, responsibilities, needs, problems, and interaction with the AMX service and system.

## 2. Stakeholder Summary

| Stakeholder                   | Interest                               | Priority |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | -------- |
| AMX Owner/Admin               | Operate and grow AMX                   | High     |
| Visitor/Customer              | Obtain a reliable experience           | High     |
| Tour Guide                    | Receive customers and provide services | High     |
| Driver                        | Provide transportation                 | High     |
| Hotel/Lodge                   | Receive guests and referrals           | High     |
| Boat Provider                 | Provide boat services                  | High     |
| Tour Operator                 | Provide tourism services/partnership   | High     |
| School/University Group       | Organize group trips                   | Medium   |
| NGO/Company                   | Organize group experiences             | Medium   |
| Tourism Authority/Association | Regulatory/industry interests          | Medium   |

## 3. Stakeholder Details

### 3.1 AMX Owner/Admin

**Needs:**

* Receive and manage inquiries.
* Manage customers and providers.
* Check provider availability.
* Create quotations.
* Manage bookings.
* Track payments.
* Manage follow-ups.
* Monitor revenue and performance.

**Problems to solve:**

* Manual coordination.
* Scattered customer information.
* Difficulty tracking bookings.
* Pricing and availability confusion.

**System interaction:** Full administrative access.

### 3.2 Visitor/Customer

**Needs:**

* Discover available experiences.
* Understand package details.
* Submit trip requirements.
* Receive a clear quotation.
* Confirm a booking.
* Receive support.
* Provide feedback.

**Problems to solve:**

* Uncertainty about prices.
* Difficulty coordinating multiple providers.
* Uncertainty about availability.
* Lack of one responsible contact.

**System interaction:** Public website and inquiry/feedback functions.

### 3.3 Tour Guide

**Needs:**

* Receive tour opportunities.
* Provide availability.
* Provide service prices.
* Understand customer requirements.
* Receive booking information.

**Problems to solve:**

* Inconsistent customer flow.
* Communication difficulties.
* Unclear trip requirements.

**System interaction in MVP:** No direct account. AMX communicates manually.

### 3.4 Driver

**Needs:**

* Receive transport requests.
* Provide availability and price.
* Understand pickup and destination information.

**System interaction in MVP:** Manual communication through AMX.

### 3.5 Hotel/Lodge

**Needs:**

* Receive guest referrals.
* Provide room availability and prices.
* Coordinate accommodation requirements.

**System interaction in MVP:** Manual communication through AMX.

### 3.6 Boat-Service Provider

**Needs:**

* Receive booking requests.
* Provide availability and price.
* Confirm service details.

**System interaction in MVP:** Manual communication through AMX.

### 3.7 Tour Operator

**Needs:**

* Provide or support complete tourism services.
* Collaborate with AMX.
* Receive customers.

**System interaction:** Partnership/manual coordination in MVP.

### 3.8 Group Customers

Examples:

* Schools
* Universities
* NGOs
* Companies

**Needs:**

* Group transportation.
* Coordinated activities.
* Clear group quotation.
* Reliable scheduling.
* One responsible contact.

## 4. Stakeholder Expectations

AMX should provide:

* Clear communication.
* Accurate pricing.
* Reliable coordination.
* Transparent booking information.
* Appropriate provider verification.
* Responsive customer support.
* Clear cancellation conditions.

## 5. Stakeholder Influence

### High Influence

* AMX Owner/Admin
* Key tourism partners
* Relevant regulatory/industry stakeholders

### High Service Impact

* Visitors

* Hotels

## 6. Requirements Validation Responsibility

The AMX Owner/Admin will be responsible for final business approval.

Stakeholders will provide feedback according to their area of involvement.

## 7. Open Questions

The following must be validated during elicitation:

* Provider verification requirements.
* Provider pricing and commission models.
* Customer payment preferences.
* Cancellation and refund practices.
* Legal requirements.
* Hotel and transport coordination process.
* Customer communication preferences.

## 8. Phase Status

This document is initially based on project assumptions.

Stakeholder interviews must be completed before final requirements are approved.
