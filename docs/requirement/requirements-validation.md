# AMX Requirements Validation

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how AMX requirements will be reviewed before system design and implementation.

## 2. Validation Criteria

Every requirement should be:

### Correct

It represents a real business or user need.

### Complete

Important information and conditions are included.

### Consistent

It does not conflict with another requirement.

### Feasible

It can reasonably be implemented with available resources.

### Testable

A test can determine whether the requirement has been satisfied.

### Clear

It has only one reasonable interpretation.

### Traceable

It has a unique identifier and can be connected to its source and related system artifacts.

### Relevant

It contributes to AMX business or user objectives.

## 3. Validation Questions

For every requirement ask:

* Why is this required?
* Who needs it?
* Is it necessary for the MVP?
* Can it be implemented?
* Can it be tested?
* Does it conflict with another requirement?
* Is it within project scope?
* What happens if it is not implemented?

## 4. Validation Status

Each requirement will receive one status:

* Draft
* Under Review
* Validated
* Approved
* Rejected
* Changed
* Deferred

## 5. Review Participants

Primary reviewer:

**AMX Project Owner**

Additional reviewers:

* Relevant tourism providers
* Potential customers
* Developer/system architect
* Relevant legal or operational advisors when required

## 6. Phase Gate

Requirements Engineering is ready for approval when:

* Major stakeholder needs are understood.
* Requirements are documented.
* Requirements are prioritized.
* Conflicts are resolved.
* Major assumptions are validated.
* MVP scope is clear.
* Requirements are testable.
* Traceability is established.
