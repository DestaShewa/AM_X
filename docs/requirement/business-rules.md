# AMX Business Rules

## BRL-01 — Provider Verification

A provider shall not be represented as "Verified" until the defined AMX verification process has been completed.

## BRL-02 — Provider Availability

Provider availability should be confirmed before including the provider's service in a final quotation where applicable.

## BRL-03 — Quotation Required

A customer should receive a quotation before a booking is confirmed.

## BRL-04 — Quotation Total

A quotation must contain a clearly calculated total price before it is sent to the customer.

## BRL-05 — Booking Confirmation

A booking shall not have the status `Confirmed` until the customer has accepted the quotation and required confirmation conditions are satisfied.

## BRL-06 — Booking Reference

Each confirmed booking shall have a unique booking reference.

## BRL-07 — Payment Recording

Every recorded payment shall contain amount, date, payment method, and status.

## BRL-08 — Cancellation

A cancelled booking shall retain its cancellation record and reason.

## BRL-09 — Price Changes

Provider price changes shall be reflected in quotations before customer confirmation.

## BRL-10 — Customer Data

Customer information shall only be accessible to authorized personnel who need it for AMX operations.

## BRL-11 — Financial Transparency

Customer-facing prices shall clearly identify important included and excluded costs.

## BRL-12 — Provider Responsibility

Each service shall have an identified responsible provider.

## BRL-13 — Service Scope

AMX shall not promise a service that has not been confirmed or reasonably verified.

## BRL-14 — Review Integrity

Customer reviews shall not be published or used as verified testimonials without appropriate moderation.

## BRL-15 — MVP Operational Model

Provider communication may remain manual during the MVP.

## BRL-16 — Legal Compliance

AMX operations shall comply with applicable Ethiopian tourism, business, tax, consumer, payment, and data-protection requirements as determined before commercial operation.
