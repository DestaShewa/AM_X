# AMX Functional Requirements

## 1. Public Website

### FR-01

The system shall display AMX information and services.

### FR-02

The system shall display available tourism packages.

### FR-03

The system shall display package details, inclusions, exclusions, duration, and conditions.

### FR-04

The system shall provide AMX contact options.

## 2. Inquiry Management

### FR-05

The system shall allow visitors to submit inquiries.

### FR-06

The system shall collect required travel information.

### FR-07

The system shall assign a unique identifier to each inquiry.

### FR-08

The administrator shall be able to view inquiries.

### FR-09

The administrator shall be able to update inquiry status.

## 3. Customer Management

### FR-10

The administrator shall be able to create customer records.

### FR-11

The administrator shall be able to update customer information.

### FR-12

The administrator shall be able to view customer inquiry and booking history.

## 4. Provider Management

### FR-13

The administrator shall be able to create provider records.

### FR-14

The administrator shall be able to update provider information.

### FR-15

The administrator shall record provider services and pricing.

### FR-16

The administrator shall record provider verification status.

### FR-17

The administrator shall record provider availability information.

## 5. Package Management

### FR-18

The administrator shall be able to create packages.

### FR-19

The administrator shall be able to update packages.

### FR-20

The administrator shall define package services, duration, pricing, inclusions, and exclusions.

## 6. Quotation Management

### FR-21

The administrator shall create quotations from customer requirements.

### FR-22

The system shall calculate quotation totals.

### FR-23

The administrator shall record included and excluded services.

### FR-24

The administrator shall record quotation validity and cancellation conditions.

### FR-25

The administrator shall change quotation status.

## 7. Booking Management

### FR-26

The administrator shall create a booking from an accepted quotation.

### FR-27

The system shall assign a booking reference.

### FR-28

The administrator shall update booking status.

### FR-29

The administrator shall record booking cancellation.

## 8. Payment Management

### FR-30

The administrator shall record payment information.

### FR-31

The system shall record payment amount, date, method, and status.

### FR-32

The administrator shall view outstanding balances.

## 9. Follow-Up

### FR-33

The administrator shall record customer follow-ups.

### FR-34

The administrator shall record follow-up dates and notes.

## 10. Reviews and Complaints

### FR-35

The administrator shall record customer reviews.

### FR-36

The administrator shall record complaints and their resolution status.

## 11. Authentication and Security

### FR-37

The system shall provide secure administrator authentication.

### FR-38

The system shall restrict administrative functions to authorized users.

### FR-39

The system shall record important administrative actions where required.

## 12. Reporting

### FR-40

The system shall provide basic operational information such as inquiries, bookings, payments, and revenue.

## 13. Communication

### FR-41

The system shall provide links or mechanisms for contacting customers/providers through supported communication channels.
